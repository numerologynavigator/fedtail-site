FILL IN, THEN UPLOAD
1. Open index.html, privacy-policy.html and terms.html in Notepad and replace:
   [DEVELOPER NAME]  -> the developer name shown on Google Play
   [CONTACT EMAIL]   -> the public contact email you want users to see
   (use Find and Replace, "Replace all")
2. Upload every file in this folder to the root of your site.
3. Check https://YOURSITE/app-ads.txt shows the single line starting with google.com.
