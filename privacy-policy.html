Fedtail – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
Developer: Navrang Labs
Contact: anandnaidu0999@gmail.com

Summary: Fedtail has no accounts and never uploads your pet-care data. Your pets, routines and care log stay on your phone. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, which uses a few technical identifiers described below. Fedtail Pro removes the ads.

What the app stores (on your device only)

Pet names and types, care routines and reminder times, care log entries (what was done, when, and which person's name you typed), optional care notes, and settings such as language. This data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use: your device's advertising ID and app-set ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), app interactions (such as launches and taps on ads) and diagnostic information (app and SDK performance). This data is sent to Google over an encrypted connection. We do not receive your care data in this process, and we do not send it to advertisers. See Google's explanation: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent before ads are requested, using Google's consent message. You can change your choice later in More → Privacy and ad settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Ads are limited to a family-friendly content rating.

Purchases (Fedtail Pro)

Pro can be bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns whether a Pro purchase is active so it can remove ads and unlock features.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

When you tap Share or Export, Fedtail creates a message, image, PDF, CSV or "household file" and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp or email). From then on that app's own privacy policy applies. A household file contains the data listed above, so only share it with people you trust.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad SDK in the free version.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later): to show the reminders you set. Optional.
  • Receive boot-completed / time-change events: to restore your reminders after a restart. No data is read.

Fedtail does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Children

Fedtail is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Backups and deleting your data

Android cloud backup is turned off for this app. To remove your care data, delete the pets in the app or uninstall it. To move to a new phone, use "Send household file" on the old phone and "Merge a household file" on the new one. Ad-related data held by Google is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Not medical advice

Fedtail is a simple care log. It does not provide veterinary or medical advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address. Questions: anandnaidu0999@gmail.com.

Pet photos

If you add a photo of your pet it is copied into the app's private storage on your phone. It is never uploaded and is not part of any file you share.
